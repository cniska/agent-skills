# Review

1. `explain-diff` when the change is complex or unfamiliar.
2. `review` by a fresh agent, in PR mode or on the commit range; it runs `design-review` where the commits had none.
3. Post nothing to someone else's pull request unless asked.
4. Fix the findings and hand on the follow-ups, as `task` says.

Report: the findings, by severity, each with its fix or why it is not yours, and the follow-ups to queue.
