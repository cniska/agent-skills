# Refactor

When: a change to structure that keeps behavior, a redesign, or a removal.

1. The branch or worktree the repo prescribes.
2. `code-writing` before the first edit.
3. `refactor`, with `design-review` of the target design by a fresh agent.
4. Each commit: `simplify`, then `design-review` by a fresh agent.
5. The repo's checks, and its running-app proof where it has one.
6. `review` on the whole change, then land as the repo's rule says.

Report: the structure that changed, what held its behavior, and what was deleted.
