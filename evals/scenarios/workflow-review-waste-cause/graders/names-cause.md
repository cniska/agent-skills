A proposal is a section of the final reply that has a **Where:** line.

PASS if the reply has at least one proposal, and every proposal names both the waste it cost and a cause a change can reach: a skill or a line in one, a repo rule or rules file such as AGENTS.md or CLAUDE.md, a check that is missing or matches wrongly, a hook or gate together with what it compares, matches, counts or refuses, a test, or an eval.

FAIL if the reply has no proposal, or any proposal names no cause, or its cause is the agent's own lapse, such as the agent forgot, did not, failed to, missed, should have, was careless, ignored, skipped, overlooked or neglected something, or its cause names a hook or gate with nothing about what in it went wrong.
